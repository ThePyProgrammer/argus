<objective>
Close the remaining Phase 6 semantic verification gaps after 06-04.

Purpose: The runner currently computes the action from pre-step info, but labels the just-completed step from post-step info. At a command-schedule transition, the first boundary row can claim `[0.4, 0.0, 0.0]` even though `env.step(action)` received `[0.0, 0.0, 0.0]`. This plan makes step rows audit the command that actually drove each step and adds real-env dispatch coverage for the velocity-command transition path.
Output: A targeted runner regression, an evaluation-loop fix, and an ArgusGo2Env fake-MuJoCo dispatch regression.
</objective>
